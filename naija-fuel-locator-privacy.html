This policy explains how the Naija Fuel Locator Android app (package com.greyorigin360.naijafuellocator), developed by Grey Origin 360 Ltd (“we”, “us”), handles information when you use it. The app helps drivers in Nigeria find petrol (PMS), diesel (AGO), CNG, kerosene (DPK) and cooking gas (LPG) near them.

Information the app uses

Your location

With your permission, the app reads your device’s precise or approximate location while it is open, to show filling stations around you and their distance. Location is not collected in the background. We do not run servers that receive or store your location. To answer a search, your coordinates (or the area you are looking at) are sent to the map and place services listed below. You can refuse or withdraw location permission at any time in your phone’s settings and still search for places by name.

Places you search for

When you type a place, area or street into the search box, the text you type and your approximate location (to show nearby results first) are sent to Google Places or OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim service to suggest matching places. We do not keep a record of your searches.

Fuel availability and price reports

Reports you make about whether a station has fuel and at what price are saved only on your phone. They are not uploaded to us or shared with other users.

Search balance and purchases

The app counts your free and purchased searches on your phone. When you buy a search pack, Google Play gives the app a purchase confirmation (a purchase token and the product bought) so the searches can be added. This is stored on your phone only. See Purchases.

Technical information

Like any internet request, requests the app makes to the services below include your device’s IP address and basic technical details (such as app package name and device type). Those providers handle this under their own privacy policies. The app itself does not collect device identifiers, contacts, photos, files, or any other personal information.

Who receives information

The app connects directly from your phone to these services. We do not receive copies of what is sent.

Purchases

Search packs are sold through Google Play. Your card or other payment details go directly to Google; we never see or store them. Refunds and payment questions are handled under Google Play’s policies, and you can also contact us using the details below.

Storage, security and deletion

Everything the app stores (fuel reports, your search balance and purchase confirmations) is kept in the app’s private storage on your phone. Connections to the services above use encrypted HTTPS. Because we hold no account or server copy of your data, you can delete all of it at any time by clearing the app’s storage in your phone’s settings or uninstalling the app. Clearing data may also remove unused purchased searches; contact us if this happens and we will help.

Children

The app is intended for drivers and adults buying fuel. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Nigeria’s Data Protection Act 2023, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its use. Since we do not hold personal data about you on our own systems, most of these rights are exercised by managing permissions and app data on your phone, or with the providers listed above. You can contact us with any request or question and we will reply within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts using information in a new way, for example by sharing fuel reports between users, we will update this policy and change the effective date above before the change takes effect.
